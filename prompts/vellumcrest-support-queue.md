Vellumcrest support queue

A desktop support queue for Vellumcrest, a made-up analytics product. A left sidebar with the queues Open, Pending and Solved, each with a count, and Open selected. A header with a search field and a filter button with an icon. A table of tickets with subject, requester, priority and last update. Clicking a ticket opens a panel on the right with the conversation and a reply box with a Send button. Also show the queue when it has no tickets.
