Larkboard event sign-up

A phone flow for Larkboard, a made-up events app. The first screen shows an event: its title, the date and place, a short description, a choice of ticket type (Standard or Supporter), a quantity field, and a "Get tickets" button. The second screen asks for a name and an email, with a "Pay" button that opens a confirmation dialog. The dialog's "Done" button goes back to the event.
